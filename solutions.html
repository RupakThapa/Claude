Pairings that work well

Services share the same systems and the same patients, so combining them removes hand-offs.

  • Verification + prior authorization

    For specialty and imaging practices

    The verifier spots the auth requirement while checking benefits and passes it straight to the auth specialist. The order never waits in between.

  • Virtual MA + referral management

    For primary care and multi-specialty groups

    The assistant handles inbox and scheduling while the coordinator tracks every referral in and out. One team, one set of protocols.

  • Verification + billing support

    For practices fighting denials

    Fix eligibility at the front end and work what's already denied at the back end, with both sides seeing the same patterns.

What's the same across every service

  • Your systems. Staff work in your EMR and portals under named logins you control.
  • Your hours. Shifts run in your time zone, aligned with clinic hours.
  • Your rules. We write your workflows into a playbook during onboarding, and you keep it.
  • Security. No local storage, no printing or downloads, and a signed BAA before access.
  • One contact. A named lead on our side and a weekly review with yours.
