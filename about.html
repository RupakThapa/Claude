About [BRAND]: remote staff trained for medical practices

We place trained admin staff inside U.S. medical practices to handle the screen-based work that keeps a practice paid: verification, prior auths, referrals, inbox work and billing follow-up. They work in your systems, on your hours, by your rules.

Who we are

We focus on one thing: administrative work in medical practices. We don't do general virtual assistance or call-center work, and our staff don't split time between healthcare and other industries.

How we vet and train staff

Every person we place goes through the same steps before they see a patient record.

  1. Screening

    Background and references

    Identity, background and reference checks, plus a written and spoken English assessment.

  2. Skills

    Role-specific testing

    Practical tests in the work they'll do: reading benefits, building an auth packet, working a denial.

  3. Compliance

    HIPAA and security training

    Privacy and security training before placement, with a signed confidentiality agreement and refreshers every year.

  4. Placement

    Trained on your practice

    They learn your EMR, payers and protocols during onboarding, then work under supervision until your lead signs off.

How we keep patient data in your systems

The simplest way to protect records is not to move them. Our whole setup starts from that rule.

  • Work happens in your EMRStaff use your EMR, PM system and portals directly. We don't run a parallel system.
  • Locked-down workstationsNo local storage, and printing, downloads and USB drives are disabled.
  • Credentials you manageOne named login per person, issued and revoked by your practice.
  • Agreements firstA signed BAA with your practice and confidentiality agreements with every staff member.
