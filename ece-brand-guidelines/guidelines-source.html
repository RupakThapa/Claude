01 · Brand FoundationOur Story

The road is easier
walked together.

Eastern Carolina Emmaus (ECE) is a community of Christian adults across eastern North Carolina who host the Walk to Emmaus: a three-day experience where pilgrims step out of the ordinary and meet Christ again on the road.

The name comes from Luke 24. Two disciples walk the road to Emmaus, grieving and confused, and a stranger joins them. They only recognise him when he breaks bread at their table. Our identity follows the same arc: an ordinary road, a companion alongside, an open table and a heart set alight.

After the weekend the journey continues in the Fourth Day: reunion groups, Behind the Scenes, sponsoring others and the prayer vigil. The brand serves both moments, inviting someone to their first walk and keeping the Fourth Day community connected.

“Were not our hearts burning within us while he talked with us on the road and opened the Scriptures to us?”

Luke 24:32 · NIV

02 · LogoThe Mark

A cross over the land we serve

The ECE logo is drawn like an old engraving, which makes it a natural partner for our illustration style. Every line was drawn on purpose, so the logo is always placed from the master files, never redrawn.

Anatomy

  • The wooden cross: hand-hewn and rough-grained. Christ at the centre of the walk, rising above the land.
  • North Carolina: the state outline, Outer Banks included. The cross stands in the east, where our community lives and walks.
  • The pine bough: evergreen, a nod to the longleaf pine, our state tree. It stands for faith that keeps growing through the Fourth Day.
  • The lettering: custom condensed serif letters. It is artwork, not a font, so never retype it.

02 · LogoClear Space & Size

Give the logo room to breathe

Keep a clear margin on every side of the logo equal to the height of the capital E in “Emmaus,” measured from the logo artwork itself. Text, images, page edges and other logos stay outside this zone.

Minimum sizes

The engraved cross and pine bough are finely detailed. Below these sizes the lines fill in, so use the Wordmark instead. For a favicon, a simplified mark may be needed.

05 · ImageryPhotography

Sanctuary light, real people

Our photographs feel like being there: warm natural light, candle glow, stone and wood, hands and faces in genuine moments. Close crops and shallow depth of field keep attention on what matters.

Look for

  • Hands: praying, breaking bread, serving, holding a candle
  • Stained glass, arches and light falling across a room
  • Eastern NC landscapes: country roads, fields, water at dawn
  • Fellowship: laughter, embraces, a shared table

05 · ImageryPhoto Treatment

One warm, consistent grade

1 · Natural

Straight from camera. Fine for news and event recaps.

2 · Brand warm grade

Default. Slightly warmer, softly desaturated, lifted shadows. Ask SAM IT for the preset.

3 · Burgundy duotone

For hero banners and headers with text on top. Keeps any photo on-brand.

✓ Do

  • Use ECE's own people and places, with permission
  • Keep a burgundy scrim (Deep Wine, 40–60%) under any text on photos
  • Credit photographers in captions when asked

✕ Don't

  • Never photograph or describe weekend moments kept as a surprise for future pilgrims
  • Use generic stock clichés, heavy filters, flash-lit snapshots or blurred phone crops
  • Show a pilgrim's face without consent, or any minor at all

05 · ImageryIllustration

Simple line art

ECE illustrations are simple, single-weight outline drawings with no shading or fills. They are calm and friendly, and they leave the detailed engraving to the logo. They add warmth where we have no photograph, and sit behind content as quiet watermarks.

  • Line colour: Sepia on light, Blush on burgundy
  • As a watermark: 25–40% opacity, bleeding off the edge
  • One illustration per section. Never crowd them.
